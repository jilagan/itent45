18 — Capacity · still one machine

Still one machine. Now watch those workers do the work.

Same box as panel 17, same workers. There you counted how many processes fit in the RAM; here you find out what they buy you. Requests arrive whether or not you are ready. They wait in a queue, the workers pull from it one at a time, and pushing arrivals past what they can drain makes the queue grow without bound. Latency goes vertical while throughput stays flat. Several machines only appear in panel 19.
